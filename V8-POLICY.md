# V8.1 — Data stewardship engineering policy

**Status:** Engineering principles approved for V8.1 documentation only. **V8.2 is blocked until the explicit decisions below are approved.** This document authorizes no runtime change, migration, retention schedule, deletion, or claim of legal compliance. It is engineering guidance, not legal advice. The proposed lifecycle and current inventory are in [`V8-DESIGN.md`](V8-DESIGN.md), especially §§4 and 8; actual columns and relationships are in `src/core/database/schema.ts`.

## Approved boundaries

- **No destructive work now.** Inventory, design, and read-only previews are permissible planning concepts; no current record is eligible for deletion or redaction under this document. No final retention window is selected, including for new guilds. Existing guilds must explicitly opt in to a reviewed policy and confirm before a first destructive pass; changing a default must never silently backdate or prune their material.
- A future eligibility clock must start at an authoritative **terminal timestamp**, not creation time or an inferred inactivity date: ticket `CLOSED` at `closed_at`, report `CLOSED` at `closed_at`, appeal `ACCEPTED`/`REJECTED` at `reviewed_at`. Reopened, open, pending, disputed, or otherwise nonterminal records are ineligible. Exact windows and any reterminalization behavior remain open.
- Any eventual worker must be **bounded, tenant-scoped, transactionally guarded and resumable**: guild/status/timestamp-filtered eligibility, small batches, a guarded row lock or equivalent, tenant fencing, concurrency checks against reopen/review/hold changes, deterministic FK-safe order and idempotent payload redaction rather than indiscriminate parent deletion. Provide an authorized per-guild **dry-run preview and explicit confirmation** before execution, plus a stop switch and reviewed recovery/fix-forward path. Never use unbounded scans or Discord API calls inside pruning transactions.
- Preserve minimal provenance and create **metadata-only receipts** (actor, guild, domain, record reference, policy version, count, timestamp and outcome as appropriate), without deleted narratives, transcript text, attachment URLs, raw user text, secrets or SQL. Preserve lifecycle/status and accountability evidence where redaction suffices; NOT NULL payloads require an approved schema/sentinel design rather than improvised empty strings. Authorization and receipt/audit failure semantics require review before implementation.
- **Holds and unresolved work always override age.** Future authorized holds make a record ineligible while active; no scheduler may remove a hold or treat an expired/ambiguous hold as permission to delete without the approved rule. Active cases/appeals, open tickets/reports, active events/giveaways, cooldown/replay windows and outstanding reservations remain protected. Automation `UNCERTAIN` evidence is never auto-deleted or blindly retried: an external Discord action may have occurred and needs evidence-based human reconciliation (`src/modules/automation`, `V7-VALIDATION.md`).
- **Copies have independent lifecycles.** PostgreSQL redaction does not erase Discord posts/channels, transcript downloads, local exports or existing backups. Backup ownership, encryption/access, expiry and restore-time reapplication of erasure decisions need separate approved procedures; Discord-copy handling needs its own scoped policy and cannot be promised by database deletion. The bot must not own backup uploads or embed dump credentials (`V8-DESIGN.md` §5).

## Working classification, not an erasure authorization

| Class | Examples and engineering treatment |
| --- | --- |
| **HIGH PRIVATE** | Closed ticket transcripts and attachment URLs; report description/evidence/resolution; appeal reason/review text; moderation staff-note **content**; verification-sensitive decisions. Minimize access; consider payload-only redaction after terminal state, authority and holds are resolved. `src/core/database/schema.ts` ticket/report/appeal tables. |
| **ACCOUNTABILITY** | Moderation cases/reasons, staff-note authorship/provenance, dashboard audit, governance receipts and Automation execution/uncertainty evidence. Preserve defensible status/provenance; staff-note content and case reasons need separate stricter review, not automatic case hard-deletion. `src/core/database/schema.ts`, `src/dashboard/web.ts`. |
| **PUBLIC COMMUNITY** | Suggestions/posts/votes, events and giveaways with entries/results, role-menu published panels and their Discord copies. Public visibility does not erase participant privacy or pending-result/replay obligations; DB and Discord copies require separate handling. `src/core/database/schema.ts`. |
| **LOW-CONTENT PERSONAL** | Analytics identifiers/aggregates, anti-raid join signals, XP/reputation/replay state, session metadata, AI metadata and other member-linked operational IDs. Low content is not anonymous; distinguish transient subject rows from aggregate/accountability records and preserve active anti-abuse bounds. `src/modules/analytics/repository.ts`, `src/modules/ai/retention.ts`, `src/core/database/schema.ts`. |

A subject request must inventory references by guild and distinguish the requester's eligible state from other people's reports, active abuse controls and accountability evidence. Neither a guild-admin action nor a subject request implies global one-click disappearance.

## OPEN BEFORE V8.2 — explicit approval decisions

1. **Retention windows and start/restart semantics:** select separate terminal-state windows for ticket transcript/attachments, report narratives/evidence and reviewed appeal narratives, including minimum/maximum policy bounds, new-guild defaults, historical-record opt-in, reopened records, and effective policy version. The candidate ranges in `V8-DESIGN.md` are proposals **not adopted windows**.
2. **Authority and coapproval:** name the policy owner/controller boundary, live Eiren ADMIN role for each guild action, who may preview/confirm/change policy or redact, whether owner or independent second-person coapproval is mandatory (especially evidence and historical records), and how authority changes/revocation are recorded.
3. **Hold authority and lifetime:** define who can place, review, extend and release holds, permitted reasons, evidence minimization, scope (record/subject/guild), expiration versus mandatory review, audit trail and how conflicting holds block work. No default expiration or automatic release is approved.
4. **Moderation cases and staff notes:** decide separate treatment of case reason, sanctions, pending appeals, notes and authorship; specify whether any note-content redaction is permitted, who approves it, and which provenance must remain. Do not authorize case hard-delete by inheriting private-ticket windows.
5. **Subject identity verification:** determine off-platform requester verification, guild/subject association, impersonation protection, administrator review, third-party evidence boundaries, denial/hold explanation and receipt access; do not expose other people's private text to prove identity.
6. **Backup retention and restore:** assign operator ownership, encryption/access controls, backup rotation/expiry and deletion limits, restore-time reapplication of outstanding redactions/holds, and what claims can actually be made about old copies. Do not invent a recovery or backup-retention duration.
7. **Discord-copy default and opt-in:** decide whether Discord messages/channels or exported/downloaded copies are untouched by default or handled through a distinct opt-in workflow; establish authority, feasibility, missing permissions, ambiguous API results and separate receipts. Never imply a database redaction retracts a Discord copy.
8. **Execution and proof:** approve exact dry-run output, confirmation/coapproval flow, hold-race and tenant-lock protocol, batch limits, receipt fields and audit-write failure behavior, redacted sentinel/schema strategy, stop/restart behavior and test criteria before any V8.2 migration or implementation.

**Gate:** Until all applicable decisions are explicitly approved and recorded, V8.2 closed-private-content lifecycle work remains blocked: do not add its deletion/redaction migration, enable a retention worker, or process live guild material. V8.1 documentation is not approval for any destructive operation. Existing authorized domain workflows and established bounded maintenance remain unchanged.
